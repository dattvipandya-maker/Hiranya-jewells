Jewellery Care & Hallmarking

BIS (Bureau of Indian Standards) hallmarking certifies the purity of gold jewellery sold in India. Every HIRANYA gold piece is hallmarked and carries a unique HUID (Hallmark Unique Identification) number you can verify.

Store pieces separately in soft pouches or a lined box to prevent scratching — gemstones and gold both mark easily against each other. Keep away from direct sunlight and humidity.

Our Everyday collection is built for daily wear. For finer bridal and Polki pieces, we'd recommend saving them for occasions — see our full Jewellery Care guide for metal- and stone-specific advice.

Sizing

Wrap a strip of paper around your finger, mark where it overlaps, and measure the length in millimetres — our Size Guide (linked from any ring's product page) converts that to a HIRANYA size.

We'd recommend sizing up — a ring that's slightly loose is more comfortable long-term than one that's tight, and resizing up later is simpler than sizing down.
