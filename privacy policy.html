Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Information We'd Collect

In a live version of this store, creating an account, placing an order, or joining the newsletter would collect: your name, email address, phone number, shipping address, and order history. Payment details would be handled entirely by a licensed third-party payment gateway — HIRANYA's own systems would never store card numbers, CVVs, or UPI credentials.

2. How We'd Use It

  • To process and ship orders, and to communicate about their status.
  • To respond to messages sent through the Contact page.
  • To send newsletter updates, only if you've opted in — and you'd be able to unsubscribe at any time.
  • To improve the site based on aggregate, anonymized browsing patterns.

3. What This Demo Actually Stores

In this project as built, the following live only in your browser's local storage, on your device, and are cleared if you clear your browser data:

  • Items in your bag and wishlist.
  • Demo account details you type into the Sign In / Register form (not verified against any real system).
  • Demo orders placed through Checkout.

None of the above is sent to a server, logged, or accessible to anyone but you, on your own device.

4. Cookies

This site does not set tracking or advertising cookies. localStorage is used instead of cookies for cart/wishlist/account state, and is not shared across sites or sent with network requests.

5. Third-Party Services

This page loads fonts from Google Fonts and styling from the Bootstrap CDN — both standard, publicly hosted static assets. In a live version, a payment gateway (e.g. Razorpay or similar) would process payments directly and would have its own separate privacy policy.

6. Your Choices

You can clear everything this site has stored at any time by clearing your browser's site data for this domain, or by using your browser's developer tools to clear localStorage.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy (or about the project itself) can be sent through the Contact page.
