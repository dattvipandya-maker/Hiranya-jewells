Keep It Beautiful

Jewellery Care

Fine jewellery ages well when it's looked after — a few minutes of care extends a piece's life by decades. Here's how we'd care for each material.

boltGold

  • Wipe with a soft, lint-free cloth after each wear to remove oils and skin contact residue.
  • Avoid contact with perfume, lotion and hairspray — apply these before putting jewellery on.
  • Rose and yellow gold can be gently cleaned with lukewarm water and a drop of mild soap.
  • Remove before swimming — chlorine can discolor gold alloys over time.

diamondDiamonds

  • Soak in warm water with a few drops of mild dish soap, then brush gently with a soft toothbrush.
  • Diamonds attract oil — clean more often than other stones if worn daily.
  • Have prongs checked annually; a loose setting is the most common cause of a lost stone.
  • Store separately — diamonds can scratch other jewellery, including other diamonds.

spaColoured Gemstones

  • Emeralds and opals are softer and more porous — avoid soap, heat and ultrasonic cleaners entirely.
  • Wipe with a barely damp soft cloth only; never soak.
  • Keep out of direct sunlight for extended periods — some stones can fade.
  • Remove before any activity involving impact — sports, gardening, heavy lifting.

circlePearls

  • Put pearls on last, after perfume and hairspray have dried completely.
  • Wipe with a soft cloth after wear; never use jewellery cleaner or ultrasonic cleaners.
  • Store flat in a soft pouch — pearls can lose their luster if left in a sealed plastic bag.
  • Restring a frequently-worn pearl necklace roughly once a year.

Storage

One Piece, One Pouch

Every HIRANYA order ships with an individual soft pouch — use it. Storing pieces loose together, even in a lined box, is the single most common cause of scratches on both metal and stones.

Keep your collection somewhere dry and out of direct sunlight. Humidity accelerates tarnish on silver-alloyed white gold, and prolonged sun exposure can fade certain gemstones.
