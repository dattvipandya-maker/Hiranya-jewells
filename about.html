Since the Atelier's First Bench

Heritage, Held Lightly

HIRANYA — from the Sanskrit for gold — began as a single karigar's bench and a belief that heirloom craftsmanship didn't need heirloom weight. Every piece we make still passes through the same hands-on process; only the silhouettes have grown quieter.

Our Story

Two Cities, One Bench

HIRANYA was founded on the idea that a piece of jewellery should carry the weight of its craft, not the weight of its gold. Our karigars trained in Jaipur's old kundan workshops; our design language was shaped by a decade spent between Mumbai ateliers and European ready-to-wear studios.

What emerged is deliberately in-between: traditional joinery and stone-setting techniques, restrained into shapes that don't compete with what you're already wearing.

Heritage

What We Keep From the Past

Kundan setting, temple motifs, and meenakari enamelling on the reverse of a piece — details no one sees but the person wearing it — are not decorative choices. They're the techniques that separate an heirloom from an accessory, and we haven't found a reason to abandon them.

Where a modern silhouette calls for it, we simplify the setting or lighten the gold weight. We never simplify the technique itself.

Craftsmanship

Every HIRANYA piece passes through the same five hands before it reaches yours.

01

Design & CAD

Every silhouette is modelled in 3D before a single gram of metal is cast, so proportions are settled before craftsmanship begins.

02

Hand-Setting

Stones are set by hand under magnification — the same kundan and prong techniques used in Jaipur's workshops for generations.

03

Finishing & Hallmarking

Each piece is polished, quality-checked, and BIS hallmarked before it's photographed — nothing ships without both.

Sustainability

Gold That's Been Gold Before

A meaningful share of the gold we work with is recycled from existing jewellery, refined to the same purity as newly mined metal. Our diamonds are sourced through suppliers who follow the Kimberley Process, and every gemstone above 0.5ct ships with its own certification.

We'd rather make fewer pieces well than more pieces quickly — which is, in the end, its own kind of sustainability.

Authenticity, in Writing

Every HIRANYA piece ships with BIS hallmarking, a signed certificate of authenticity, and — for diamonds above 0.5ct — independent gemological certification. Nothing about what you're wearing should be a matter of trust alone.
